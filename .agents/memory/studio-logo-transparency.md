---
name: Studio logo transparency
description: Intent and boundaries of automatic studio-logo background cleanup
---
Studio logos should retain their actual silhouette rather than display an opaque white image rectangle on dark pages.

**Why:** The user reported the white square around an uploaded circular mark as an unwanted placeholder. White details inside the mark must remain intact; the uploaded source must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Limit cleanup to logo presentation/export, not moodboard photos. Preserve already-transparent artwork and enclosed white details. Do not apply a circular crop, blending mode, or global white-color removal to arbitrary logo shapes.
